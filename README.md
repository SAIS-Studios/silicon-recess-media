# Silicon Recess public media

Free educational newsletter and promotional media about practical school AI decisions. Research, writing and narration are AI-assisted.

[Silicon Recess newsletter](https://siliconrecess.beehiiv.com/subscribe) · [Media releases](https://github.com/SAIS-Studios/silicon-recess-media/releases)

## Publishing and credentials

The integration serves the owner’s Buffer account. It reads connected account, channel and post identifiers and creates or schedules posts on approved channels. It does not request direct-message, comment or account-settings access.

When configured, OAuth tokens and publishing credentials are stored only on the owner’s Stewartmini computer. They are excluded from this repository, public media and content snapshots. Public files include newsletter copy, videos, captions and graphics. Private research evidence, review records, configuration and quality reports are excluded.

Public content may be copied or retained by GitHub, Buffer, social platforms and other external providers. [Media privacy information](media-privacy.html).
