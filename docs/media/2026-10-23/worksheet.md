# AI Practice Suggestion Review Map

Use this original proposed guide to map where AI practice suggestions go in your school and draft a review step for team discussion. It is a starting point, not a tested procedure.

1. List the AI tools in your building that suggest what students practice next.
2. For each tool, note whether suggestions go straight to students or wait for a teacher.
3. Pick one tool and propose who could review its suggestions.
4. Propose a review window that keeps practice moving.
5. Note how any written insight about student reasoning will be checked against actual work.
6. Bring the draft to a team meeting and record what colleagues want to change.
