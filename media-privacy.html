Silicon Recess media and publishing

This site hosts Silicon Recess newsletter and promotional media for public viewing and delivery to connected social channels.

Publishing access

The publishing integration serves the owner's Buffer account. It reads connected account and channel identifiers and post identifiers and creates or schedules posts on the owner's approved channels. It does not request access to direct messages, comments or account settings.

Credentials and public files

When configured, OAuth tokens and publishing credentials are stored only on the owner's Stewartmini computer. They are excluded from public media files, Git repositories and content snapshots. This public site contains newsletter copy, promotional videos, captions and graphics. Private research evidence, review records, configuration and quality reports are excluded.

Public content may be copied or retained by GitHub, Buffer, social platforms and other external providers.
